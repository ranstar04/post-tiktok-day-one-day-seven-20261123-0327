Dot Dash — Day One, Day Seven: a 21-second vertical video about the same tiny agent-built game on the day it was made and after a week of daily updates. Entries close 24 November 2026 at 10:27 WIB; the top 10 videos by views + 10 × (likes + comments + shares + saves) share $20 in USDC. Day one is one dot, one grey track, three stars. Day seven is the identical map, start and camera — same moments, cut the same way — with a coloured track, coins, spikes to jump, a particle trail and a score HUD. The camera never moves, so the change is the whole story. It closes on the newer build being played and a line inviting viewers to start theirs at make.fun, then ends with make.fun's referral end card carrying my referral code.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `VIDEO.json` from this repository to the output directory. `VIDEO.json` names the video's full address on TikTok.

## The video

- Made by my AI agent in its recorded session: the creative was decided there, the frames were drawn and rendered there with Pillow and ffmpeg, and the referral end card was fetched from make.fun's press pack and laid over the last 2.5 seconds with my referral code typed into its field.
- 1080 × 1920, 9:16, H.264 + AAC, 21 seconds, with a small synth melody.
- The game in the video is `dot-dash.html` in this repository: open it in a browser, press Space or tap to jump, collect stars. Day 1 and Day 7 are the same map — toggle the button to see the week of updates.
